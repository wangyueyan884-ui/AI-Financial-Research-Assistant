# Product Requirement Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Name

AI Financial Research Assistant

---

## 2. Product Overview

AI Financial Research Assistant is an AI-powered financial research tool that helps analysts efficiently analyze research reports, extract key insights, and generate structured investment reports.

The product combines Large Language Models (LLM) and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) technology to improve financial research efficiency.

---

## 3. Background

Financial analysts need to process large amounts of research reports, company documents, and industry information every day.

Traditional workflows require significant time for:

- Reading lengthy documents
- Extracting key information
- Summarizing investment insights
- Comparing industry trends

AI technology provides an opportunity to improve research efficiency and decision support.

---

## 4. Target Users

### Primary Users

Financial analysts and investment researchers.

### User Characteristics

- Need to analyze large amounts of financial documents
- Require accurate and structured insights
- Value efficiency and information organization

---

## 5. User Pain Points

### Pain Point 1: Long research documents

Users spend significant time reading lengthy reports.

### Pain Point 2: Information extraction

Important insights are difficult to identify quickly.

### Pain Point 3: Report generation

Analysts need to manually summarize and organize findings.

---

## 6. Product Goals

### User Goals

- Quickly understand research reports
- Ask questions about documents
- Generate investment insights

### Product Goals

- Reduce research time
- Improve analysis efficiency
- Provide structured AI assistance

---

## 7. Core Features

## Feature 1: Dashboard

Description:

Provides users with an overview of research tasks and analysis entry points.

Key Functions:

- Research overview
- Industry information
- Document analysis entrance


## Feature 2: AI Chat Analysis

Description:

Users interact with AI assistant to analyze uploaded research documents.

Key Functions:

- Upload research reports
- Ask questions
- Receive AI-generated answers


## Feature 3: Report Generation

Description:

Generate structured financial analysis reports based on AI analysis.

Key Functions:

- Report summary
- Industry insights
- Investment analysis

---

## 8. AI Technology Solution

### LLM

Provides:

- Natural language understanding
- Reasoning capability
- Content generation


### RAG

Provides:

- Financial document retrieval
- Knowledge grounding
- Reduced hallucination


### Prompt Engineering

Provides:

- Structured output
- Financial analysis templates
