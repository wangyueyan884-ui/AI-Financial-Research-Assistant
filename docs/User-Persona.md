# User Persona

## Persona 1: Financial Analyst

### Basic Information

**Name:** Alex

**Role:** Financial Analyst

**Industry:** Investment Research

**Experience:** 2-5 years


---

## Background

Alex works in an investment research team and is responsible for analyzing industry trends, company performance, and financial reports.

Daily tasks include reading research reports, extracting important information, and preparing investment analysis materials.


---

## Goals

- Quickly understand large amounts of financial information
- Extract key insights from research reports
- Improve research efficiency
- Generate structured analysis reports


---

## Pain Points

### 1. Time-consuming document reading

Financial reports are often dozens or hundreds of pages long, requiring significant reading time.


### 2. Difficult information extraction

Important investment insights are distributed across different sections of reports.


### 3. Repetitive analysis work

Analysts spend considerable time summarizing reports and creating research notes.


---

## User Behavior

- Reads multiple research reports every week
- Frequently compares information across documents
- Uses tools to improve research efficiency
- Needs reliable and structured information


---

## User Needs

| Need | Solution |
|---|---|
| Quickly understand reports | AI summary |
| Ask questions about documents | AI Chat |
| Generate analysis materials | Report Generation |
| Retrieve relevant information | RAG-based search |
