# AI Financial Research Assistant

An AI-powered financial research assistant designed to help analysts analyze research reports and generate investment insights.

## Product Overview

AI Financial Research Assistant is an AI product that combines Large Language Models (LLM) and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) technology to improve the efficiency of financial research analysis.

The product helps users quickly understand research reports, ask questions about documents, and generate structured investment analysis reports.

---

## Problem

Financial analysts spend significant time reading, summarizing, and extracting insights from large amounts of research documents.

Traditional analysis workflows are time-consuming and difficult to scale.

---

## Solution

This product provides:

- AI-powered research report analysis
- Intelligent document Q&A
- Automated investment insight generation
- Structured financial reports

---

## Target Users

- Financial analysts
- Investment researchers
- Research associates

---

## Core Features

### 1. Dashboard

### 2. AI Chat Analysis
